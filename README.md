### University Research & Innovation Dashboard
**Goal:** Create a dashboard that displays research papers and patents coming out of Research Universities in the United States from 2019 to 2026. 

**Code:** [University Research & Innovation Dashboard Code.ipynb](https://github.com/SabrinaJoner1/Data-Analytics-Portfolio/blob/main/University%20Research%20%26%20Innovation%20Dashboard%20Project/University%20Research%20%26%20Innovation%20Dashboard%20Code.ipynb)

**Description:** This dashboard is organized into three primary analytical components: a **Heat Map** section, a **Keyword Search** section, and a **University Output Over Time** section. Each component includes configurable filters that allow users to refine the display of papers and patents based on selected criteria.

The **Heat Map** section provides three distinct visualizations: a papers heat map, a patents heat map, and a combined map integrating both data sources. Users can hover over any university to view the total number of papers and/or patents associated with that institution. When a university is selected, a detailed results table appears beneath the map, displaying papers and patents that match the currently applied filters for the chosen university.

The **Keyword Search** section includes a detailed results table that surfaces individual papers and patents. Users can apply multiple filters, including a keyword query, to narrow the dataset and focus on specific research topics, institutions, or categories.

The **University Output Over Time** section presents a series of line charts, one for each university, illustrating annual output across multiple research categories. This view enables users to identify trends, compare category‑level activity, and observe how each institution’s research focus evolves over time.

**Skills:** Data cleaning and data visualization

**Technology:** This dashboard was created in Python using pandas, dash, ploty.express, json, unicodedata, re, and ast.

**Results:** The [Result](https://github.com/SabrinaJoner1/Data-Analytics-Portfolio/blob/main/University%20Research%20%26%20Innovation%20Dashboard%20Project/Result.md) a dashboard that has three different sections that allow you to use different filters to see the differences over time in papers and patents at different research universities. 


### 2024 Amazon Book Sales Tableau Project 
**Goal:** Analyze Amazon’s 2024 book marketplace to identify trends in pricing, ratings, review behavior, genre performance, and popular authors and books.

**Code:** [BookSaleDashbroad](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/sabrina.joner/viz/BookSaleDashbroad/2024BookSales) Project on Tableau Public.

**Description:** This Tableau project explores a dataset of 4,847 books sold on Amazon in 2024, using aggregated metrics to understand how different genres perform across key consumer indicators. The story is organized into six analytical sections: **Introduction, Genre Ratings Distribution, Price vs. Rating, Ratings vs. Review Count, Highest Priced Books,** and **Popular Books and Authors**.

The analysis begins with the **Genre Ratings Distribution**, which highlights how reader sentiment varies across 100 genres and identifies categories with consistently strong or mixed reviews. The **Price vs. Rating** section then examines how average price aligns with these rating patterns, revealing which genres command higher prices or diverge from expected trends. Next, the **Ratings vs. Review Count** view compares perceived quality with overall engagement to distinguish genres with broad audiences from those with more niche followings. The **Highest Priced Books** section isolates the top priced title in each genre, surfacing categories with premium pricing or notable outliers. Finally, the **Popular Books and Authors** section showcases the top 50 titles and authors by review volume, providing insight into market reach, consumer interest, and commercial impact.

Across all sections, the project uses genre-level aggregation, LOD expressions, and interactive visualizations to uncover patterns in consumer behavior and book performance.

**Skills:** Data cleaning, data visualization, exploratory data analysis, Tableau storytelling, LOD calculations, aggregation logic.

**Technology:** Tableau, calculated fields, LOD expressions, interactive dashboards, and story point design.

**Results:** A multi-section Tableau story that visualizes pricing trends, rating distributions, engagement patterns, and popular authors and books across genres, providing a comprehensive analytical overview of Amazon’s 2024 book marketplace.
